# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This is a scientific repository. Everyone taking part — reporting an issue,
reviewing, contributing code, or discussing results — is expected to keep the
exchange technical, specific and civil.

Behaviour that is welcome:

- Reporting what you observed, with the command and the output.
- Disagreeing with a claim by pointing at evidence.
- Saying plainly when something is wrong, including your own work.
- Asking for clarification instead of assuming intent.

Behaviour that is not:

- Personal attacks, insults, or comments about a person rather than the work.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or bad-faith argument.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages, and any
other space where the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainer at the address in [`README.md`](README.md).
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions that do not meet this standard, and may block accounts for
repeated or serious violations.

Because scientific disagreement is the point of the exercise, a strongly
worded technical objection is not a violation. An attack on the person making
it is.
